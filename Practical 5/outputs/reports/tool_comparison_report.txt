================================================================================
KEY METHODOLOGICAL TAKEAWAYS:
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Domain Heuristics vs Generic AutoML:
   Automated tools (DataRobot, H2O, TPOT) excel at mathematical transformations
   (powers, logs, PCA, interaction terms), but CANNOT intuitively invent cybersecurity
   concepts such as Shannon entropy, SQL syntax signatures, or sliding login windows
   without human domain knowledge.

2. Performance on 1.49M Records:
   Executing commercial cloud tools or exhaustive TPOT genetic searches across 1.49M
   rows is resource-prohibitive. Highly optimized vectorized numpy/pandas routines
   complete full dataset transformations in seconds while preserving raw data integrity.
